A quick guide for parents

Start with daily practice, or choose a short walk. Ask your child to explain a method and check the answer. Use a hint when needed; supported work counts as practice, separately from independent accuracy.

For a paper-style session, open Desk Mode and choose SET multiple choice or NWSSEE written answers. You can skip, flag and return before submitting. Print a worksheet and its separate answer sheet for practice on paper.

The NWSSEE has written English and Maths papers, each lasting 40–50 minutes. Only the final maths answer is marked. Our original questions are practice materials; follow the school’s instructions for your child’s test.
